Cambridge International & Education

Cambridge International Education (CIE)


Cambridge International Education (CIE), a division of the University of Cambridge, is a leading global provider of international qualifications for students aged 3 to 19. It offers the Cambridge Pathway, a comprehensive educational framework divided into four stages: Cambridge Early Years, Cambridge Primary, Cambridge Lower Secondary, and Cambridge Advanced. Each stage focuses on developing critical thinking, creativity, and in-depth knowledge across a broad range of subjects. Qualifications such as the Cambridge IGCSE and Cambridge International AS & A Levels are recognized by universities and employers worldwide for their academic rigor and quality. CIE also collaborates with governments and educational institutions to enhance curriculum design, assessment methods, and teacher training, promoting excellence in education globally.

A distinctive feature of this program is its focus on skill-building and critical thinking, encouraging students to apply knowledge in practical and real-world contexts. Cambridge Lower Secondary also provides structured assessments, including the Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint tests, which evaluate students' progress and help identify areas for improvement. These assessments are internationally benchmarked, ensuring that students receive a globally recognized measure of their achievements.

Assessment plays a key role in the program, with tools such as the Cambridge Lower Secondary Checkpoint providing an internationally recognized evaluation of students’ progress.

These assessments offer detailed feedback on strengths and areas for improvement, empowering educators and parents to support each student’s unique learning journey effectively.

One of the unique features of the program is its emphasis on Cambridge Global Perspectives, a skills-based subject that encourages learners to explore global issues critically and collaboratively

Beyond academics, the program emphasizes the importance of personal and social development. It helps students cultivate values like resilience, teamwork, and empathy, preparing them to be global citizens in an increasingly interconnected world. With its flexible structure and international recognition, Cambridge Lower Secondary is an ideal choice for schools aiming to provide learners with a high-quality education that aligns with global standards.
