Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE)


The Federal Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE), founded in 1975, is a prestigious educational body that oversees Secondary School Certificate (SSC) and Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC) education in Pakistan. Operating under the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training, FBISE ensures quality standards nationwide through rigorous curriculum development, standardized assessments, and comprehensive examination systems. The board serves educational institutions across federal territories, cantonments, and Pakistani schools abroad, maintaining consistent educational standards that prepare students for higher education and professional careers.

FBISE is renowned for its commitment to educational excellence, implementing modern teaching methodologies and assessment techniques that evaluate both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. The board regularly updates its curriculum to align with international standards while preserving national educational values and cultural context. This balanced approach ensures that students receive an education that is both globally competitive and locally relevant.

  • Comprehensive curriculum covering sciences, humanities, and technical subjects.
  • Standardized examination system ensuring fair assessment nationwide.
  • Regular teacher training programs to enhance educational quality.

Beyond academics, FBISE promotes holistic development through co-curricular activities, character building, and civic education. The board encourages critical thinking, problem-solving, and ethical values, preparing students to become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society. With its forward-thinking approach and commitment to continuous improvement, FBISE remains at the forefront of educational excellence in Pakistan.

FBISE provides a solid educational foundation that prepares students for success in higher education and professional life.
