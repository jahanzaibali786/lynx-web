WWF Pakistan


WWF Pakistan, established in 1970, is the country's leading environmental conservation organization, working to protect Pakistan's biodiversity, promote sustainable resource use, and combat climate change. As part of the global World Wide Fund for Nature (WWF) network, it focuses on science-based solutions to preserve wildlife, forests, freshwater ecosystems, and marine environments. The organization operates across Pakistan, collaborating with communities, government agencies, and businesses to balance ecological needs with human development.

WWF Pakistan is renowned for its holistic approach to conservation, implementing projects that address habitat protection, species recovery, and sustainable livelihoods. Key initiatives include the conservation of endangered species like the snow leopard and Indus River dolphin, reforestation campaigns, and advocacy for climate-resilient policies. Through education and community engagement, WWF Pakistan empowers local populations to become stewards of their natural heritage while promoting green economic practices.

  • Biodiversity conservation through protected areas and species recovery programs.
  • Sustainable resource management in forestry, fisheries, and agriculture.
  • Climate change mitigation and adaptation strategies.

Beyond conservation, WWF Pakistan drives systemic change through policy advocacy and corporate partnerships. It works to reduce ecological footprints, promote renewable energy, and improve water governance in critical ecosystems like the Indus Basin. The organization also leads public awareness campaigns on environmental issues, inspiring youth and communities to adopt sustainable lifestyles. Its international network facilitates knowledge exchange and global conservation efforts.

WWF Pakistan bridges the gap between ecological preservation and human progress, ensuring nature thrives alongside development.

Dr. Masood Arshad
